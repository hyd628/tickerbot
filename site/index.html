Built for how you actually watch prices

Two kinds of alerts, a transparent multi-source price pipeline, and settings that never leak between chats.

Six chains, one bot

Solana, Sui, Ethereum, Bitcoin, Hyperliquid and Robinhood's tokenized-stock platform — by coin id or on-chain address.

Multi-source pricing

Pyth first, then PreStocks, then CoinGecko — whichever actually has the asset. Every price shown names its source.

Threshold watches

/watch alerts on every move past a percentage you set, then resets its baseline — built for ongoing monitoring.

One-shot price targets

/limitalert fires exactly once when a token reaches the level you name, above or below, then removes itself.

Tokenized stocks

Watch PreStocks' pre-IPO equities (OpenAI, SpaceX, Anthropic) and Robinhood's tokenized stocks by ticker, same as any coin.

Private by chat

Watch ids and default intervals are scoped per chat — they never reveal how many watches or users exist elsewhere.

Where it looks for prices

Pick a chain, give it a coin id or a contract/coin-type address.

Command cheatsheet

Everything the bot responds to, straight from /help.

/watch chain id price% [label]Start a recurring percentage-threshold watch.
/limitalert chain id price [label]One-time alert when price reaches a level, then auto-removes.
/defaultWatch BTC, ETH and SOL at 1% in one shot.
/listShow your active watches.
/pricesLive price of everything you're watching, deduped.
/price chain idCheck any price on demand, watched or not.
/unwatch idStop watching (id from /list).
/setinterval [minutes]Your default polling interval, per chat.
/setinterval id [minutes]Override the interval for one watch.
